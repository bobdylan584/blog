Idioms/Collocations: 
What are Idioms- Idioms are fixed phrases/expressions in English, where the meaning of each individual word in that phrase has a separate meaning to that fixed phrase. 
 
-a shoulder to cry on- a person who shows sympathy and support to someone in need. 
-to be in dire straits-to be in a very serious or bad situation. 
-a last resort-to be the last option or choice available. 
-a vicious cycle- a problem that leads to more problems which then leads to the first problem all over again. 
-grasping at straws-to be desperate and to hold on to even the smallest bit of hope that you can find in that situation. 
-the tip of the iceberg- it's just a small part of the problem from which more is to follow. 
-to dodge a bullet- to barely escape from a bad/dangerous situation. 
-catch 22- a situation that is hard to succeed in because of other elements that conflict with it. 
-between a rock and a hard place-to be in a difficult situation and to have to choose between 2 equally undesirable solutions
