Workplace related causes of conflict :
Conflict – A serious disagreement or argument.
Inconsistent Management – When leaders make decisions that change often or are unfair.
Power Struggles – Fights over who has control or authority.
Lack of Resources – Not having enough money, tools, or people to do the job you need to do

Personal characteristics as causes of conflict: 
Envy – Wanting what someone else has (jealousy) 
Boredom – Feeling tired and uninterested because nothing is exciting.
Personality Clashes – When people don’t get along because they have very different ways of thinking or acting.

Modals of Advice: are verbs used to give suggestions or recommendations about what someone should or could do.
Must – Strong necessity or obligation.
Should – Strong advice or recommendation.
Could – Suggests possibility or a softer recommendation.
Might – Indicates possibility or uncertainty.

Structure: They are always followed by the base form of the main verb.
Modal + Base Verb
Must + resolve
Might + be
Should + try
Could + find
